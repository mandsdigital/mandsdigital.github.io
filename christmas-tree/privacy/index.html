🎄 Privacy Policy

Christmas Tree Decoration · by M&S Digital

Effective November 2, 2026

The short version: Christmas Tree Decoration collects no personal information at all. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and your game progress stays on your own phone. It is made to be safe for children.

1. Information we collect

None. The game:

  • does not ask you to sign up or sign in;
  • does not ask for your name, email, age, location, contacts, photos or microphone;
  • has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services;
  • does not send any information about you over the internet.

2. What stays on your phone

To remember your place in the game, the app saves on your phone only: the levels you finished, your stars, your lives and when they refill, sound settings, and which tips you have already seen. This stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is never sent anywhere. You can delete it anytime by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Children

Christmas Tree Decoration is for players of all ages, including children under 13. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. This is in line with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Google Play's Families policy and the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, email us and we will help right away.

4. Third parties

We share nothing with anyone, because we collect nothing. The game contains no third-party services that gather data. The app is downloaded from Google Play, which has its own privacy policy.

5. Future updates

We may add optional features later, such as ads suitable for children or in-app purchases protected by a parent check. If we do, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety section before that version is released, and explain what is collected, why, and who receives it.

6. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
