🎄 Privacy Policy

Christmas Tree Decoration · by M&S Digital

Effective November 4, 2026

The short version: We (M&S Digital) collect no personal information. There are no accounts, and your game progress stays on your own phone. This app shows ads, provided by Google AdMob. The ads are set up for children: they are child-directed and non-personalized, and the app does not use the advertising ID. To show an ad, Google may process some technical information, as explained below.

1. What we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. The game:

  • does not ask you to sign up or sign in;
  • does not ask for your name, email, age, contacts, photos or microphone;
  • has no analytics or crash-reporting services of our own;
  • never sends your game progress anywhere.

2. What stays on your phone

To remember your place in the game, the app saves on your phone only: the levels you finished, your stars, your lives and when they refill, sound settings, and which tips you have already seen. This stays on your device. We cannot see it, and it is never sent anywhere. You can delete it anytime by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Ads

This app shows ads so the game can stay free. The ads come from Google AdMob, and we have set them up for a young audience:

  • Child-directed. Every ad request is tagged as directed at children, so Google treats it under its rules for children's apps (including COPPA).
  • Non-personalized. Ads are not based on your interests or on what you do in other apps.
  • No advertising ID. The app does not use the device's advertising ID; it is blocked in the app.
  • Rated for general audiences (G).
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You choose whether to watch one for a bonus (such as an extra life). The game works without them.

To deliver an ad, measure that it was shown and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may process:

  • the device's IP address, which can show an approximate location (such as the country or city);
  • the device's app set ID, an identifier that is not used for advertising, for limiting how often the same ad is shown, measuring ads and preventing fraud;
  • device information, such as the device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how the app is used with ads, such as when an ad was shown, tapped or closed, and basic diagnostic information.

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that show its ads at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive any of this information about you.

4. Children

Christmas Tree Decoration is for players of all ages, including children under 13. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are child-directed and non-personalized, as described above. This is in line with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Google Play's Families policy and the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

5. Parents' rights

If you are a parent or guardian:

  • You can ask us at any time what information the app handles about your child, and we will explain it. We hold none ourselves.
  • You can delete everything the game saved on the phone by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
  • You can stop all ads by uninstalling the app.
  • For information Google processes to show ads, you can contact Google through its privacy policy page.
  • Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, COPPA and similar laws, you have the right to be informed, to object, and to ask for access, correction or deletion. Email us and we will help right away.

6. Other third parties

Apart from Google AdMob (above), the game contains no third-party services that gather data, and we share nothing with anyone. The app is downloaded from Google Play, which has its own privacy policy.

7. Future updates

We may add other optional features later, such as in-app purchases protected by a parent check. If we do, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play Data safety section before that version is released, and explain what is collected, why, and who receives it.

8. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
