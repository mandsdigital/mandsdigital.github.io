Privacy Policy

El Niño Ph Heat Stroke Tracker · by M&S Digital

Effective October 31, 2026 · Updated November 4, 2026

This policy explains what information the El Niño Ph Heat Stroke Tracker app ("the app") uses, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The app is made by M&S Digital ("we", "us") in the Philippines. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

In short: the app works without an account. Your city, settings and family contacts stay on your phone. We do not use your GPS location, and we do not read or send your text messages. Ads and in-app purchases are handled by Google. If you choose Pro Family Alerts, we keep the family-circle details needed to send those alerts, and you can delete them at any time.

1. Information that stays on your phone

These are saved only on your device. We cannot see them.

InformationWhy
The city or municipality you chooseTo show its heat index and forecast
Settings (language, text size, alert level)To remember your preferences
Family contacts you add (name and mobile number)To prepare heat-warning text messages for you to send
Saved forecast and newsSo the app works offline

This information is removed when you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Location

The app does not ask for or use your phone's GPS or location permission. You pick a city from a list.

Text messages and calls

When you tap Text, Share or Call 911, the app opens your phone's own messaging, sharing or dialer app with the message or number filled in. You decide whether to send or call. The app cannot send messages by itself and cannot read your messages, contacts or call history.

Notifications

Morning heat alerts are scheduled on your phone from the forecast. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.

2. Information sent to other services

Weather data

To get the forecast, the app (or our server, which stores forecasts for all users) requests weather data from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway) for the coordinates of the city you picked — not your own location. Like any website, their servers see the internet (IP) address the request comes from. See MET Norway's terms.

News

Headlines are loaded from Google News. Opening a story takes you to the publisher's website, which has its own privacy policy.

Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. A report contains technical details only: what went wrong in the app, the app version, your phone model, Android version and a random installation ID. It does not include your name, contacts, family members, messages or location. See Firebase's privacy information.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version and the Lifetime Plan show ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, device and app information, an approximate location based on your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Shopping links (Shopee and Lazada)

The Heat Essentials section contains affiliate links. If you tap one, Shopee or Lazada may record that you came from our app, and we may earn a commission if you buy something. We do not receive your name, address or payment details. Purchases are covered by those stores' own privacy policies.

Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

The Lifetime Plan and Pro Family Alerts are bought through Google Play. Google handles your payment details; we never see them. To check which plan you have, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random app user ID, the purchase receipt from Google Play, and basic device information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

3. Pro Family Alerts (only if you sign in)

Automatic alerts to your family need a small online service, so this feature requires signing in with your Google account (through Google Firebase Authentication). If you use it, we store:

InformationWhy
Your name, email address and Google account IDTo sign you in and identify your family circle
Family members you add: name, city, birth year, and optional risk factors (outdoor worker, pregnant, chronic illness)To decide at what heat index each person is at risk and when to alert the family
Family circle membership and invite codesSo members can join your circle
"I'm OK" check-ins and alert historyTo show the family who has responded
A notification token for each phoneTo deliver alerts (via Google Firebase Cloud Messaging)

Risk factors such as pregnancy or chronic illness are health-related information. They are optional, you choose whether to add them, and they are used only to set alert thresholds. Only the full birth year is stored, not the full birthdate. Please add another person's details only if they (or, for a child, their parent or guardian) agree.

This information is stored on our server, which runs on Cloudflare, and is visible only to members of the same family circle. It is not sold and not used for advertising.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your GPS location.
  • We do not read your messages, contacts, photos or files.
  • We do not use family-circle or health-related information for ads.

5. How long we keep information

  • On your phone: until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall.
  • Pro Family Alerts account: until you delete your account. When you do, we delete your account and family-circle data within 30 days.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as needed to manage your purchase and meet legal and tax duties.

6. Your choices and rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access, correct and delete your personal information, to object to its processing, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. You can:

  • Delete family contacts and settings in the app at any time.
  • Delete your Pro Family Alerts account inside the app (Settings → Account → Delete account), or email us to do it for you.
  • Turn off notifications and reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

7. Security

Information sent between the app and online services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to our server is limited to M&S Digital. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children

The app is meant for adults, including parents and guardians, and is not directed to children under 13. Parents may add a child as a family member so the family can be alerted about them; in that case only the child's name, city and birth year are stored.

9. Health information disclaimer

The heat index is calculated from forecast temperature and humidity and classified using PAGASA heat index levels. The app gives general heat-safety information based on Department of Health advice. It is not medical advice and is not a medical device. In an emergency, call 911. The app is not affiliated with PAGASA, the DOH, or any government agency.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date, and tell you in the app if the changes are significant.
